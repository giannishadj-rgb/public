# Independent luxury art-direction review — 17 October 2026

**Initial verdict: FAIL for full luxury-house parity.** This is a convincing premium storefront with a strong opening, clear proposition and unusually specific evidence. I would not yet sign off an unqualified claim that the whole homepage belongs beside La Prairie, Chanel, Clé de Peau Beauté and Augustinus Bader. The remaining gap is the strength and coherence of its photographic story, especially the missing visual culmination of its four-product promise.

Fresh Chromium inspection: actual local homepage at 1440 × 950 and 390 × 844. Read PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md; no previous review report was consulted. No website code or assets were changed by this reviewer. Section screenshots can include the fixed navigation at an artificial vertical position caused by Playwright's element screenshot framing; that is not reported as a website defect. ## 1. Five-second proposition — PASS

Both initial viewports communicate all five required ideas without scrolling: Swiss origin, caviar skincare, four formulas, one complete ritual and a science-led proposition. The headline, two short support lines and Complete Ritual action create a very clear reading order. The chrome cream jar is recognisably skincare and makes the page feel expensive immediately.

## 2. Hero composition and immediate desire — PASS

The supplied hero earns its area. Desktop balances the copy against a large, tactile jar; mobile has a deliberate top-to-bottom sequence of message, object and action. The warmth, reflections and restrained interface create a coherent entrance. The mobile purchase action fits inside the observed initial viewport. There is no need to replace the approved hero or decorate it with additional claims, badges or motion.

The satin/chrome vocabulary is recognisably luxury beauty rather than uniquely Swiss, but the copy gives it enough brand relevance. Preserve this opening while strengthening what follows it.

## 3. Typography, interface restraint and house identity — PASS

The upright/italic serif and quiet sans-serif are controlled. The collection is unboxed, fine rules are used sparingly, and the warm light palette continues into the footer. The design avoids crowding the four-product range with catalogue furniture. It is already more restrained than the promotional overlays observed on some reference sites.

The small silver desktop mark recedes beside the large campaign image and wide navigation. A modest increase in its desktop optical size could improve house recognition; preserve the supplied mark and its colours. This is a refinement, not the principal reason for the parity failure.

## 4. Photographic coherence and credible provenance — FAIL

The caviar macro is strong: its scale, dark polished forms and pale ground make the ingredient memorable. The current heritage image, however, shows a branded jar in a gloved hand beneath a filling nozzle in a laboratory-like setting. Beside “Created and filled in Zug” and ISO wording, it reads like evidence of the real manufacturing process. The small “Formulation study” caption does not make that implication disappear. I cannot verify that this depicts the actual facility or unchanged real packaging from the rendered page.

The ritual portraits share a broadly warm palette but feel selected from separate shoots: shoulder/product detail, resting face with serum, eye-area portrait, then smiling face above the jar. Their crops, gestures and emotional register vary. This gives the lower homepage less campaign authority than its opening.

**Immediate improvement:** replace the unverified manufacturing scene with a non-product material image of cream, glass or steel, clearly presented as an illustrative material study. Do not manufacture documentary provenance or rebuild packaging. **Asset brief for the next campaign:** one coherent four-frame application series, with consistent light, background and skin treatment, including a woman plausibly within the primary mature audience. No age claim is being inferred about the current models.

Evidence: `desktop-caviar.png`, `desktop-heritage.png`, `desktop-ritual.png` and their mobile equivalents.

## 5. Four formulas as one desirable purchase — FAIL

The collection itself passes: four clear objects, numbered roles, benefits, quantities, prices and actions are visible. Mobile preserves comparison with a readable two-column arrangement. The failure is the final proposition: the homepage repeatedly directs visitors to the Complete Ritual but never presents all four formulas together as the set they are being asked to buy. The initial desktop closing spends approximately 855 pixels on a slogan, link and service lines. It has scale but no new product argument.

**Strongest composition change:** turn that closing into a composed four-real-product ensemble with “The Complete Ritual”, a concise all-four-full-size inclusion statement, the current WooCommerce set price and a separate-purchase comparison only while true. Keep one primary action and the shipping/returns line. Use the existing accurate product artwork without inventing a box, altering pack pixels or assuming how the cleanser is packed. Desktop can balance an ensemble against copy; mobile should show the group before the price and action. This gives the four-formula promise a visual culmination and converts empty scale into desire.

The ritual section also promises a use sequence while its images show product holding. Application imagery would make those instructions more convincing; the text is already useful and should stay visible.

Evidence: `desktop-collection.png`, `mobile-collection.png`, `desktop-closing.png`, `mobile-closing.png`.

## 6. Scientific authority and evidence presentation — PASS

The product names, methods, panel sizes and duration make the results more credible than bare percentage claims. The self-reported satisfaction figure is visibly secondary to instrument-measured results. The page does not frame these as a study of the complete ritual. The Protect/Repair/Rejuvenate explanation is calm and connects actives to relevant concerns.

Mobile science is a long reading interval. The next meaningful editorial improvement would be to make the leading sentence under each mechanism more concise while retaining the observed product-specific evidence and moving fuller mechanism explanations to the dedicated science pages. Do not inflate results or import competitor awards and endorsements that this brand does not possess.

Evidence: `desktop-evidence.png`, `mobile-evidence.png`. This is a review of visible evidence framing, not independent validation of the underlying studies.

## 7. Mobile rhythm and observed benchmark parity — FAIL for parity; PASS for basic composition

At 390 pixels, the opening, two-column collection and readable instructions work. No horizontal overflow was measured. The lower-page journey remains a long sequence of text, packshots and separate portrait panels before a text-only closing; the image-led opening is more resolved than the ending. The Complete Ritual composition is the most productive remedy. Keep useful information and natural scrolling; introducing carousels or compulsory effects would not close this gap.
