## 2. Hero composition and immediate desire — PASS

The supplied hero earns its space. The desktop composition balances copy against a large tactile cream jar. ## 3. Typography, restraint and house identity — PASS

The serif/sans relationship is controlled, genuine italic is used selectively, and the light material palette continues into the footer. Collection objects remain unboxed. Fine rules and open space give the interface structure without competing with photography. The page no longer relies on oversize closing typography alone to signal luxury.

A minor future refinement remains possible: the desktop silver mark has less optical presence than the wide navigation and campaign. A modest increase in desktop size could aid recognition while preserving the supplied artwork and colours. This is not an approval blocker and is less valuable than the composition changes already made.

## 4. Photographic coherence and provenance — PASS

The final material study of glass, water, stone and light belongs to the same warm reflective world as the hero and caviar macro. Its “A study in light and precision” caption correctly frames it as a material expression. It contains no invented branded container and does not purport to document a manufacturing facility.

The new mature application portrait supplies the human counterpoint the page needed. Visible skin texture, a calm gesture and a quiet background feel relevant to the primary audience. One image now establishes a coherent ritual mood while the numbered text explains the four separate actions; the image is not presented as photographic proof of every step.

The earlier laboratory-like branded filling scene and four disconnected product-holding portraits were material weaknesses. Their replacement is a substantive improvement, not surface polish. No material photographic defect remains in the final observed homepage.

## 5. Four formulas as one desirable purchase — PASS

The main collection remains clear: four objects, numbered roles, individual benefits, quantity, price and action. Mobile retains a readable two-column comparison.

The final closing now presents all four authentic pack images together, names the Complete Ritual, states that it includes four full-size formulas, and shows **€450** with **€500 when purchased individually**. One action and the shipping/returns line complete the decision. This is the strongest practical change in the revision: the page finally visualises the exact offering its hero asks visitors to discover.

Repeating the four products here has a different purpose from the collection. The collection explains individual choice; the closing gives the complete range a clear purchase identity. Its restrained ensemble is appropriate to the brand. It does not invent gift-box contents or make an unverified claim about how the cleanser is packed.

Evidence: `final-desktop-collection-viewport.png`, `final-desktop-closing-viewport.png`, `final-mobile-closing-viewport.png`. Prices are the values observed during this review, not a permanent price assertion.

## 6. Scientific authority and evidence presentation — PASS

The shortened mechanism copy reads more decisively, particularly on mobile. Protect, Repair and Rejuvenate are easy to scan without redundant lists of actives. The independent single-formula findings retain product attribution, method, panel and duration. Instrument-measured results dominate; self-reported satisfaction remains secondary. The homepage does not imply that the full ritual was studied together.

This is a credible balance between desire and explanation for an unfamiliar luxury skincare house. Dedicated science pages remain the appropriate place for fuller mechanisms. This review evaluates the visible framing and does not independently validate underlying research reports.
