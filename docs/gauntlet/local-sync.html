The Ritual

Always in this order, from the lightest texture to the richest.

  1. Cleanse Step 1: Cleanse. Sweep a soaked cotton pad over face and neck to lift make‑up and the day’s impurities. No rinsing: the skin is ready for what follows. (Micellar Water All-in-One Cleanser)
  2. Treat Step 2: Treat. The most concentrated step. Press two or three drops into face and neck, then give them a minute to absorb. (Advanced Repair Serum)
  3. Revive Step 3: Revive. Care for the thinnest skin of the face. Pat a little along the orbital bone, from the inner to the outer corner. Never drag. (Revitalizing Eye Cream)
  4. Restore Step 4: Restore. The final step seals the ritual. Warm a pea‑sized amount between your palms and press it in, under SPF by day and overnight by night. (24h Regeneration Cream)
