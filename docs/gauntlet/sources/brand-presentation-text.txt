WHY CAVIAR A Flagship Ingredient We know that caviar is an expensive dish, which we enjoy in special occasions accompanied by fine French champagne. At the same time, however, caviar is a basic component of cosmetology as it has the ability to erase the signs of time from mature skins and restore their natural glow. About thirty years ago, particularly in 1983, cosmetology made the first acquaintance with caviar.  Bassed  in an investigation by Mrs. Erika  Bauerpu , she demonstrated the beneficial effect of caviar on the firmness of the skin. Thanks to the high concentration of  vitelin  and the appearance of the Caspian fishermen who were without wrinkles, although they were in the sun, the idea that caviar helps the skin was born. The composition was based on sturgeon eggs and additionally contained mussels, oysters, shells, algae and plankton, in combination with vitamins, lipids, proteins, minerals and trace elements. Ingredients essential and valuable to the structure of cells. Real caviar comes from the Sturgeon, a fish living in Russia and parts of Asia. They lay millions of eggs in the rivers. The young fish live in the freshwater for some years before they go to the open sea. Today about 98% of the worlds Caviar comes from Sturgeons living in the Caspian Sea. Sevruga caviar is one of the highest priced varieties of Caviar and it is harvested from the Sevruga sturgeon native to that from the Caspian Sea.
