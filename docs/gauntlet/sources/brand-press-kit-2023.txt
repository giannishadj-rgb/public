W H Y C A V I A R
A F l a g s h i p I n g r e d i e n t
We know that caviar is an expensive dish, 
which we enjoy on special occasions 
accompanied by fine French champagne. 
At the same time, however, caviar is a basic 
component of cosmetology as it has the 
ability to erase the signs of time from mature 
skins and restore their natural glow.
About thirty years ago, particularly in 1983, 
cosmetology made the first acquaintance 
with caviar. Based on an investigation by 
Mrs. Erika Bauerpu, she demonstrated the 
beneficial effect of caviar on the firmness of 
the skin. Thanks to the high concentration 
of vitelin and the appearance of the Caspian 
fishermen who were without wrinkles, 
although they were in the sun, the idea 
that caviar helps the skin was born. The 
composition was based on sturgeon eggs 
and additionally contained mussels, oysters, 
shells, algae and plankton, in combination 
with vitamins, lipids, proteins, minerals and 
trace elements. Ingredients essential and 
valuable to the structure of cells.
Real caviar comes from the Sturgeon, a fish 
living in Russia and parts of Asia. They lay 
millions of eggs in the rivers. The young fish 
live in the freshwater for some years before 
they go to the open sea. Today about 98% 
of the worlds caviar comes from Sturgeons 
living in the Caspian Sea. Sevruga caviar is 
one of the highest priced varieties of Caviar 
and it is harvested from the Sevruga sturgeon 
native to that from the Caspian Sea.

The skin on our neck and décolleté is naturally 
different and thinner from the skin on our face, 
and one of the first places which show signs of 
aging, such as loss of elasticity, change in texture, 
discoloration and more. Less fatty tissue and 
collagen along with fewer oil glands make these 
areas more prone to chronic dryness, wrinkles and 
loss of firmness, meaning that extra care is required 
in order to rescue our neck and décolleté from the 
negative effects of time. 
This pampering and luxurious velvety HIGH 
PERFORMANCE NECK & DECOLLETE CREAM 
instantly absorbs to help re-contour the chin, neck 
and décolleté for a slimmer, firmer look.
A fortifying and restructuring agent works to 
smooth the look of roughness while improving skin 
moisturization and elasticity. A blend of powerful 
actives preserves skin integrity and protects skin 
elasticity against degradation. 
The skin immediately regains its youthful look, 
unveiling a radiant, silky smooth décolleté.
