HYALURONIC ACID  
The magnet for moisture  
Hyaluronic Acid is a high-molecular mass 
substance with strong moisturizing and 
film-forming properties. The hyaluronic 
acid film helps preserve the most important 
characteristics of youthful, healthy skin, 
such as suppleness, elasticity and tone.
Hyaluronic Acid is also an effective regulator 
of the moisture content of skin subjected 
to special stresses such as the effect of UV 
irradiation, metabolic disorders, trauma or 
the ageing process. 
Due to their physicochemical properties 
as water reservoirs, swelling agents and 
lubricants, cosmetics which contain 
hyaluronic acid lead to a subjectively evident 
and objectively demonstrable improvement 
in skin condition. 
The unique ability to hold so much water 
makes hyaluronic acid invaluable to your 
skin. Hyaluronic acid acts like a sponge, 
holding vast amounts of water in the skin, 
effectively plumping out the skin and, by 
doing so, it can reduce the appearance of 
fine lines and wrinkles, while improving 
the skin’s hydration levels and texture.

SUPEROXIDE DISMUTASE
A powerful enzyme
The Stabilized Superoxide Dismutase is a 
yeast-derived, enzyme-based free-radical 
scavenger.
This powerful enzyme protects against the 
damaging effects of the environment. 
In-vitro tests have shown that it helps protect 
against lipid peroxidation by neutralizing 
the superoxide radical, providing anti-
inflammatory and anti-oxidant effects. 
In-vivo tests have demonstrated that it 
reduces UV-induced erythema.

RESVERATROL
The longevity molecule
Resveratrol is a powerful anti-oxidant in the 
skin of red grapes that has a few incredible 
anti-aging traits. It provides cellular 
protection against free-radical attack and 
shielding against environmental pollutants. 
It also regulates phytoestrogen activity 
and helps prevent the effects of premature 
ageing.
Applied topically, resveratrol helps protect 
the skin’s surface, interrupts and helps 
rebuff negative environmental influences, 
and brightens a tired-looking complexion. It 
also has significant skin-calming properties 
that may help minimize the appearance of 
redness.
Furthermore, in-vitro tests have shown that 
it has anti-inflammatory and skin lightening 
properties.

SYN®-COLL 
SYN®-COLL is a Collagen Synthesis-
Stimulating Peptide that mimics the human 
body`s own mechanism of producing collagen.
The Collagen Synthesis-Stimulating Peptide 
(Glycerin & Aqua & Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5) is 
a small peptide that mimics the human body’s 
own mechanism to produce collagen via TGF-
beta. As well as stimulating collagen I synthesis, 
this active also protects collagen against 
degradation by inhibiting the expression of 
matrix metalloproteinases MMP1 and MMP3 
and that of pro-inflammatory cytokines. 
This peptide is the alternative to collagen 
injections as it is a deeply penetrating bio-
active ingredient, which actively diminishes 
all types of wrinkles. It has skin-firming and 
moisturizing properties; it stimulates collagen 
synthesis and has been clinically proven to be 
safe and effective.
Hyaluronic Acid
Hyaluronic Acid is a high-molecular mass 
substance with strong moisturizing and film-
forming properties. It has physicochemical 
properties as water reservoirs, swelling agents 
and lubricants therefore effectively plumping 
out the skin. 
The hyaluronic acid film helps preserve the 
most important characteristics of youthful, 
healthy skin, such as suppleness, elasticity 
and tone. It is also an effective regulator of

special stresses such as the effect of UV
