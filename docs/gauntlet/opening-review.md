# Opening review — 2026-10-18

Independent visual/commercial follow-up; actual `latest-1440.png` and `latest-390.png` plus fresh live first-view screenshots `opening-review-{1440,390}.png`. Non-blind comparison with the previously inspected saved La Prairie, Clé de Peau and Bader references. No implementation edits.

## Assessment

The new opening is a meaningful improvement. The authentic dark campaign portrait provides emotional presence, skin texture and a recognisable visual counterpoint to the silver/white packaging. The four actual products now explain the proposition immediately; the earlier single cream image made the complete-ritual CTA less self-evident. The black purchase action is clearer. On mobile the headline, origin, both shopping routes and all four product types fit before the portrait starts around 575 px, so acquisition visitors can shop without scrolling through a full-screen photograph. Desktop pairs a strong photographic field with readable copy and a precisely ordered product score. No observed product deformation, clipping or unreadable controls.

Compared with the previous 8.8 result: desirability and comprehension improve together. The portrait remains genuine brand material and the layout is appropriately quieter than the Clé de Peau holiday reference. It approaches the photographic presence of the Bader reference while showing the full regimen more clearly. La Prairie retains a more ownable chromatic/product world; Caviar's black/off-white split is still a familiar editorial structure. This is not evidence that Caviar surpasses those brands as a whole.

## Three residual opportunities — none a material failure

1. **P2 / purchase context:** The primary hero asks for the complete ritual without showing its €450 price or full-size status. The price is disclosed later and on the destination, so this is not misleading. A concise full-size/price cue could help qualified shoppers understand the commitment sooner, provided the hero remains spare.
2. **P2 / visual repetition:** Four pack shots in the hero lead directly into four large pack shots in the Collection. The two groups serve overview versus benefit/price disclosure, but the consecutive repetition slightly reduces discovery. Do not remove useful products merely to eliminate repetition; a future editorial transition should earn its space.
3. **P2 / distinction:** The new opening says what the range is more effectively than why this particular house is special. The documented Aquitaine-to-Switzerland provenance and 2008 history now appear lower down. They provide specificity, but the first impression still depends on familiar fine-serif, monochrome beauty conventions. This is a residual creative ceiling, not a reason to invent stronger claims or redo a working hero.
