# Independent material review — 18 October 2026

Scope: visual assessment of supplied desktop/mobile opening and full-page screenshots, with native-size crops of collection content. Non-blind comparison with the supplied La Prairie, Clé de Peau and Augustinus Bader opening screenshots. No prior score reports or target score consulted. No interaction, accessibility, performance or checkout tests performed. This is the independent art-direction assessment, not a technical audit.

Verdict: credible premium skincare presentation; competitive in clarity and restraint, below flagship luxury in immediate product desire and proprietary substance. The portrait/caviar pairing gives the opening a real point of view. The large original packs, legible benefits, prices and practical ritual make the range understandable. The lower page increasingly reads like a very well-typeset product brochure. It does not yet sustain the material richness of the opening.

## Three priorities

1. **The first purchase request precedes a view of what €450 buys.** Desktop's entire opening shows skin and caviar but no packaging; mobile adds four links before the photographs, and the collection begins below roughly 1,200px. The reference openings all connect their promise to an actual pack immediately. Caviar's portrait remains attractive, but the complete ritual is initially an abstraction. **Smallest effective fix:** show a compact, authentic four-pack group beside the price/CTA or in a narrow area at the base of the hero. Preserve the portrait and caviar hinge; do not add a second oversized hero. On mobile the pack group should appear before the tall photo panel.

2. **Mobile requires too much travel to compare only four products.** The collection's first slide is dominated by a tall cleanser; the adjacent serum and its copy are clipped as a continuation cue. Product image, name, benefit, price and carousel controls occupy roughly 700px before the next section. Tabs make alternatives discoverable, but visual comparison still depends on repeatedly changing slides. **Smallest effective fix:** reduce the mobile image stage by about a quarter and tighten the gap below it, keeping current text sizes and controls. The aim is a full product proposition plus a clearer next-item cue within a normal phone viewport. Keep desktop's large packs.

3. **The differentiating science loses specificity at its most important point.** “Aquitaine caviar. Swiss extraction.” is promising, but the next science moment becomes three familiar benefits—firmness, hydration, antioxidant care—on another large cream-colored field. “High-Tech Complex” is a name, not evidence of why this formulation deserves this price. The long interval of text, rules and whitespace also flattens the visual journey. **Smallest effective fix:** within the existing complex section, replace one generic sentence with one verified, concrete formulation/extraction detail already supported by brand documentation, and bring the existing caviar image back as a small material detail there. If no supported detail exists, retain honest ingredient information and shorten the section rather than inventing proof. No clinical numbers or testimonials needed.
