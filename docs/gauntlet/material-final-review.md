# Final independent visual review

Reviewed current desktop `latest-1440.png` (4,157px), native `triptych-1280.png` through `triptych-1920.png`, and current `campaign-mobile-360.png`, `campaign-mobile-390.png`, `campaign-mobile-430.png`, `campaign-mobile-599.png` plus `campaign-mobile-focus-320.png`. The earlier mobile full-page capture predates the final campaign opening; its height is not presented as current evidence. Lower mobile chapters were inspected in the preceding review and are reported unchanged. Compared non-blind with the supplied actual La Prairie, Cle de Peau and Bader openings. Source authenticity/ingredient verification is reported by the parent team, not independently repeated here. No live interaction or technical tests performed.

**Keep both desktop triptych and mobile campaign. Overall: 9.0/10 for observed design.** The original counter photograph makes the actual four products protagonists. Desktop's silver packs, warm skin and glossy caviar form a persuasive material sequence; the natural dark upper field accommodates the offer without covering packs. All four inspected desktop widths retain legible text, identifiable products and a deliberate narrow ingredient panel.

Mobile now carries comparable product presence instead of reverting to a small catalogue group. At 360, 390, 430 and 599px the headline, benefit, action, full-size offer and price are readable above or beside the complete set. At 320px the inspected focus-state capture retains clear separation between text/action and packs; removing the collection arrow preserves that space. The policy bar is visible and readable, with an intentional two-line treatment at 320px. The subsequent portrait/caviar hinge restores skin and ingredient context without competing inside the purchase image. This is a meaningful first-impression improvement, not decorative polish.

The campaign now competes with the reference openings in genuine product scale, atmosphere and composition. La Prairie retains a stronger signature color; Bader integrates person and pack in one photograph. Caviar offers a credible alternative through its silver product world, skin/caviar pairing and specific formulation-led headline. The clear collection typography, dark original singles, sourced sensory quotation and complete-offer finale sustain the presentation below the opening.

**Remaining limits:** the ingredient chapter discloses familiar actives more than it establishes a distinctive formulation argument. Improving that needs genuine additional information; campaign imagery cannot supply it. Mobile now has two consecutive image moments before the collection, so adding another introductory block or expanding the material hinge would slow the path to comparison. There is no clear screenshot-based reason for another layout change. Interaction, accessibility, performance and checkout reliability still require their own evidence.
