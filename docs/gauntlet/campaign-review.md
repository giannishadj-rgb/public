# Campaign review — independent visual/commercial pass

2026-10-18. Non-blind comparison; prior scores were not read. Inspected supplied desktop/mobile campaign captures, focused ritual/ingredient captures, then independently opened the live homepage at 1440 and 390 px. Saved `campaign-review-first-*`, `campaign-review-end-*`, and fully scrolled `campaign-review-loaded-*` screenshots. This is a focused independent assessment, not a full Impeccable two-assessor audit.

## Verdict

A convincing luxury storefront with excellent restraint and a strong pearl/silver product world. The large cream hero, readable serif/sans hierarchy and authentic campaign portrait make it feel considered. The loaded final ritual group is a strong commercial conclusion. It is not yet an unmistakable flagship: its middle act is too instructional, and its brand rationale remains fairly interchangeable with other premium caviar skincare.

Full-page captures initially hid lazy-loaded caviar, portrait and ritual images. Live scrolling confirmed all three; those blanks are capture artifacts, not site defects. The fixed header inside the supplied mobile ritual element capture is also a screenshot artifact.

## Three highest-impact opportunities

1. **P1 — A long mobile information corridor.** The ingredient complex followed by portrait plus four numbered usage paragraphs occupies several screens before the next purchase offer. Both sections are individually clear, but together they shift from desire to instruction. Smallest useful improvement: compress the ingredient section into a tighter editorial facts panel and reduce the ritual's vertical spacing; retain the portrait and factual guidance. Do not add more sections.
2. **P1 — Swiss identity has little ownable substance.** “Developed and made in Switzerland” and the five-actives explanation establish category and origin, but give limited reason to prefer this house over another. Use one concise, specifically documented brand/formulation detail if the supplied materials substantiate it. If none exists, explicitly retain this as a source constraint; decorative laboratory or mountain photography would not solve it.
3. **P2 — Mobile discovery makes the entry cleanser the principal first product.** At 390 px only cleanser is fully visible; serum, eye cream and cream require advancing the rail. That is a clear sequence but weak immediate overview for a first-time €450 prospect. Consider a compact four-formula selector or explicit product names alongside the rail controls, preserving large product photography and the ritual order.

## Benchmark comparison

Actual saved La Prairie, Clé de Peau and Augustinus Bader screenshots were inspected. Caviar's hero is competitive in legibility, composure and product prominence; it is quieter than the holiday Clé de Peau reference. La Prairie's colour/product system is more immediately ownable; Bader connects product, person and a specific benefit faster. These references are single viewport captures, so this is not a whole-site superiority claim. Pearl silk and reflective jars remain familiar luxury conventions rather than a distinctive brand signature.
