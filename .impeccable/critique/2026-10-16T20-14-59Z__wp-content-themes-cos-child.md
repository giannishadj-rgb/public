**Five priorities**

**1. P1 — Replace inflated storytelling with specific, substantiated brand evidence.**

Heritage claims the highest manufacturing standard on earth and pharmaceutical-equivalent validation, laboratories and oversight. The page does not substantiate those assertions. Journal’s featured headline says caviar outperforms conventional anti-aging actives, while its introduction dismisses other ingredients as fighting skin into compliance. This voice is less assured than the homepage’s restraint.

For an unfamiliar premium brand, these statements invite skepticism precisely where the visitor is looking for reassurance.

**Fix:** build the story around the confirmed 20+ years, actual Zug manufacturing, ISO 22716, batch release, traceability and precisely attributed product studies. Show authentic people, process and archival material where available. Review retained claims against the claims register and underlying evidence. Use plain explanations of customer benefit alongside scientific detail.

Commands: `$impeccable clarify`, `$impeccable distill`.

**2. P1 — Stop making content wait for its entrance.**

After scrolling from the homepage hero, the design reviewer initially saw an empty collection viewport; the content appeared later. Shop’s opening text also appeared after its photograph. Source inspection corroborates reveal animations lasting roughly 1.2–1.8 seconds before additional staggering.

This interrupts orientation and can look like incomplete loading. Luxury should feel composed and responsive.

**Fix:** make names, benefits, prices, navigation and actions visible immediately. Reserve brief motion for enhancing already visible imagery. Remove scroll gating and long stagger sequences from shopping and reading content.

Commands: `$impeccable optimize`, `$impeccable animate`.

**3. P2 — Make choosing a formula easier, especially on mobile.**

Shop places a large campaign introduction and a ritual offer before individual formulas. On the 390 × 844 serum view, photography dominates the first screen and the name sits near the bottom. The sticky purchase bar helps, but personal suitability and texture are farther down. Homepage collection benefits are explicitly hidden at smaller widths.

**Fix:** preserve the campaign image while shortening the route to the four formulas. Keep one short benefit per product visible on phones. Put verified skin suitability and texture beside the main benefit, size and price on product pages. Let the ritual offer support the choice without making it the only prominent route.

Commands: `$impeccable adapt`, `$impeccable clarify`, `$impeccable layout`.

**4. P2 — Give service and editorial pages their own useful compositions.**

Shipping & Returns, Contact and Journal use dark, full-screen campaign introductions. Contact’s email and form, shipping answers and Journal’s articles all start below that introduction. The repeated large italic heading and scroll invitation also create a noticeable change from the light commerce pages.

**Fix:** bring contact methods and response time into Contact’s opening screen. Start Shipping & Returns with delivery, duties and returns summaries plus anchors. Start Journal with a featured article and categories. Use supporting photography at a scale appropriate to each task. Account’s clear, light sign-in layout is a better model for practical pages.

Commands: `$impeccable distill`, `$impeccable layout`.

**5. P2 — Tighten editorial structure and finish the small details.**

Ritual repeats its organising idea across the introduction, maxim, step explanations, order rationale, morning/evening recap and final offer. Science introduces multiple taxonomies and labels five links as four pillars. The logo appears softer than neighbouring text: its nominal 222w asset reports only 119 × 45 natural pixels, displayed at approximately 111 × 42. Product detail labels measure around 11.5px; the source-inspected 404 uses 10px action labels.

**Fix:** give each chapter one distinct purpose, combine repeated guidance and correct the pillar count. Export a properly detailed header logo from the approved master and provide adequate image density. Increase small functional labels while retaining the quiet hierarchy.

Commands: `$impeccable distill`, `$impeccable typeset`, `$impeccable polish`.

**Customer impact**

- **Mature premium buyer:** the specific serum result reassures her; small labels and unsupported grand claims weaken that confidence.
- **International luxury discoverer:** the photography earns attention; she needs the actual house’s story and clear ordering support to justify an unfamiliar brand.
- **Distracted mobile researcher:** the sticky purchase bar helps, but hidden collection benefits and large introductory surfaces slow comparison.

The emotional peak is the cream campaign image. Confidence grows on the product page, dips during empty reveal moments and overstated heritage passages, then recovers with clear bag feedback. Order completion was not tested. Cognitive load comes mainly from repetition and competing scientific classifications; four products do not need elaborate catalogue filtering.
