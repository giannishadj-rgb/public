## What's working

- Four products make a manageable range. Individual PDPs show distinct roles, volumes, prices and purchase actions clearly.
- Actual packaging photography and restrained homepage compositions create desire without fabricated social proof.
- Homepage evidence correctly separates measured change, self-report and illustrative portrait imagery.
- Useful concern guides acknowledge limits: dark circles cannot always be resolved with skincare; cream satisfaction is not an instrumental hydration measure; dullness has no dedicated published radiance result.
- Shipping/returns states that reasonable trial use is accepted, and identifies return-shipping responsibility. Preserve that transparency.

## Five highest-priority trust failures

### [P1] Product-use instructions disagree

**Where:** homepage ritual, `/the-ritual/`, cleanser/serum PDPs and bundle.

- Home and cleanser PDP say no rinsing required. Ritual morning says: “Apply Cleanser to damp skin; rinse with cool water to wake the circulation.” Its evening instructions prescribe double cleansing, unlike the PDP's single no-rinse cotton-pad routine.
- Home/serum PDP: 2–3 drops and approximately one minute. Ritual: 3–4 drops and 30 seconds.
- Home uses Cleanse/Treat/Revive/Restore; PDP navigation uses Cleanse/Treat/Eye/Moisturise; ritual and bundle use further variations.

**Visitor reaction:** “Which page should I follow? Is this really one considered routine?”

**Suggestion:** Approve one instruction record per product and one set of step labels. Render them consistently across every page, FAQ and concern guide. Keep evocative descriptions separate from navigation names. **Command:** `$impeccable harden`.

### [P1] Scientific results change meaning between pages

**Where:** science hub, Clinical Studies, concern guides, cream/bundle PDPs and FAQ.

- Science and concern pages say “+47% perceived firmness” while identifying cutometry; the homepage describes an instrument measurement.
- Science attributes 100% satisfaction to the full ritual. The recorded study is specific to the 24h cream; no complete-ritual study is on hand.
- Bundle FAQ says each product's clinical results were achieved within the full ritual, disagreeing with single-product attribution elsewhere.
- FAQ ties “100% satisfaction” to barrier function, although satisfaction does not itself measure barrier function. Its Day-7 +22% wording also blurs smoothness with firmness.
- Cream places the self-report under “Independently measured performance.”
- Crow's-feet depth is used to support the dark-circle story, though these are different outcomes.
- “Every claim. Every result — verifiable,” “published in full,” statistical-significance and report-availability language exceeds the actual on-site summaries inspected. No named report records or significance data were available in those views. CRO identity is explicitly undecided.

**Visitor reaction:** “Are these independent studies or sales claims that change from page to page?”

**Suggestion:** Keep one evidence record: product, endpoint, method, panel, duration, study context, permitted wording and source. Distinguish finished-product results, ingredient research and formulation rationale. Publish genuine supporting documents if available; otherwise narrow verification promises to the disclosed summaries. **Command:** `$impeccable clarify`, followed by `$impeccable harden` for shared records.

### [P1] Formula transparency exposes unresolved contradictions

**Where:** expanded cleanser INCI, key actives, ingredient detail pages, sourcing and concern guides.

- Marketing identifies Siberian sturgeon (*Acipenser baerii*), while visible INCI reads “Acipenser Gueldenstaedtii Roe Extract.” PRODUCT.md already recognises this unresolved mismatch.
- Cleanser introduction names Aloe Vera/Tamarind Seed Extract, but the displayed ingredient list does not show them. Its shared-complex description names actives not evident in that list.
- Resveratrol is grape-skin-derived in some copy and Polygonum Cuspidatum root in other copy.
- “CITES Appendix II species” sits alongside promises that the house does not use species on the “CITES endangered species register.” These descriptions need reconciliation against approved sourcing records; this review does not decide the regulatory classification.
- Claims such as “penetrate into the dermis,” “without irritation,” dermal hydration and haemoglobin mechanisms are stronger than the qualified efficacy summaries. Their support should be checked, not assumed.

**Visitor reaction:** “If the ingredient list disagrees with the explanation, what am I buying?”

**Suggestion:** Have the owner/formulation authority reconcile current packaging, approved INCI, sourcing and marketing. Do not guess a corrected Latin name or insert missing actives. Update all affected surfaces from the approved record. **Command:** `$impeccable harden` after the factual decision.

### [P1] Bundle photograph disagrees with the promised contents

**Where:** `/products/the-complete-ritual/`, live hero asset `/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/the-complete-ritual-hero-black.webp`.

The visible cleanser label says **50 ml**, while the bundle contents and cleanser PDP promise **150 ml**. Corrected assets elsewhere on disk do not change this served image. “Everything you need, in one box” also promises a packaging arrangement that PRODUCT.md marks undecided, and overstates completeness when daytime SPF remains separate.

**Visitor reaction:** “Will I receive a full-size cleanser or a smaller one? What is actually in the box?”

**Suggestion:** Serve a label-accurate image and state the confirmed full-size contents. Confirm whether the cleanser is boxed together or alongside before describing packaging. Prefer “The four-formula ritual” over unqualified “everything you need.” **Command:** `$impeccable polish` for the asset, `$impeccable clarify` for confirmed copy.

### [P1] Payment promises conflict with the actual local checkout

**Where:** Help, bundle/cart and checkout.

Help says major credit/debit cards and PayPal are accepted. The inspected checkout offers Cash on Delivery only, including after choosing the US. The merchant onboarding constraint is documented. International COD feasibility was not established, and this local finding must not be treated as proof of the deployed store's gateways.

**Visitor reaction:** “Why did the site promise PayPal if I can only pay cash on delivery?”

**Suggestion:** Make public payment copy reflect enabled methods and actual countries. Complete the owner's gateway decision before launch and verify it end to end. Do not publish payment logos for inactive methods. **Command:** `$impeccable harden`; gateway activation is an owner/account task.

## Copy examples that weaken the first-time impression

- **Generic:** “one elegant step,” “radiant, youthful skin,” “luminous anti-aging care.” They describe the category more than the particular formula. Replace with its existing concrete benefit and texture/use role.
- **Robotic repetition:** “Every claim. Every result,” repeated “actually,” “most routines,” and declaration/counter-declaration structures. Write each page around a specific question instead of a reusable rhetorical pattern.
- **Scientific-sounding but unclear:** “biomimetic cellular regeneration,” “sirtuin activation,” “periorbital calibration,” “the densest material in nature.” Explain customer meaning first and retain technical detail only where it can be supported.
- **Borrowed authority:** “Not inspired by Swiss heritage — constituted by it,” pharmaceutical-neighbour references and extreme purity claims. Specific facts about the house are more convincing than assurances of superiority.
- **Pressure toward the complete set:** “Remove one, and the others are diminished” and ritual-optimised clinical language conflict with legitimate standalone purchases. Help visitors choose one formula confidently when appropriate.

## Recommended sequence

1. Reconcile approved application, ingredient, packaging and evidence records. These require product/business truth, not creative invention.
2. Correct payment/service promises and tracking destinations; verify subscription/loyalty/quiz capabilities before advertising them.
3. Bring the bundle offer forward, clarify cart savings and improve immediate add feedback.
4. Rewrite science, heritage and sustainability around attributable facts and scoped evidence.
5. Edit journal/category/PDP repetition into specific useful language; add genuine responsible authors/reviewers where available.
6. Apply shared readable typography and coherent controls across all routes. Finish with `$impeccable polish` after trust corrections.

## Cognitive load, emotional journey and personas

Four products are intrinsically simple. Three checklist failures—minimal choices in some navigation groups, working memory across contradictory pages, and competing research/brand/purchase demands—create moderate avoidable load. PDP chunking and disclosure are helpful. Homepage desire can turn into doubt as deeper pages change the facts; the desired ending is a confident choice rather than a finished brand presentation.

- **Affluent mature buyer:** Wants calm confidence and truthful relevant outcomes; broad mechanisms and youthful restoration rhetoric do less than precise reassurance.
- **International luxury discoverer:** Attractive design draws her in; payment and landed-cost ambiguity weaken willingness to transact.
- **Mobile researcher:** Benefits help; long repeated introductions and 10px functional text increase reading demands. Mobile layout itself was not fully tested in this site-wide run.
- **Skeptical verifier:** Single-product/full-ritual shifts and contradictory ingredient identity are the strongest trust breaks.
