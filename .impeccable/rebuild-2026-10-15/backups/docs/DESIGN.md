# Design System: Caviar of Switzerland

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Ritual"**

The site is a small, unhurried daily ceremony made visible. There are four formulas and nothing extra, and every element earns its place by helping her want, understand or buy one of them. The homepage is light from top to bottom: the hero is the 24h Regeneration Cream on pearl silk, the Collection sits on gallery white, and each story section is a full-bleed photograph with its type set on the image (the caviar macro, the clinical portrait, the Matterhorn in cloud). Darkness is kept for the footer. Type pairs a fine high-contrast serif for voice with a light sans for reading, and short spaced-caps labels for wayfinding.

Restraint is the method. Elements are spaced generously and set in a narrow, near-achromatic palette; where a surface carries warmth, it is sampled from its own photograph so image and page read as one plane. The ornaments are photographs of the real objects and textures: chrome caps, pearl silk, backlit caviar pearls, a hand holding each formula. Precision shows in the details: lining numerals, hairline rules, every clinical figure named with its product, method and panel. Copy moves her before it informs her; certificates and methods live one click deeper.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light everywhere: pearl canvas (#F2F2EF), gallery white for products, photo-matched warm tones for photographic sections; dark only in the footer.
- Type on the photograph: story sections set label, heading and lede directly on the image's calm side.
- Cormorant Garamond 300 for voice, Inter 300/400 for reading, Inter 500 tracked caps for short labels.
- Square corners, hairline rules, no shadows; feathered masks instead of frames where a photograph meets the page.
- Real, label-accurate photography as the primary ornament; the ritual numerals 1–4 as the closing section's spine.

## Colors

A near-achromatic palette of pearl, white and graphite. Warmth enters only where a photograph brings it, and Swiss red is reserved for the logo.

### Primary
- **Graphite Ink** (graphite-ink): headings, primary text, filled and outlined buttons on light surfaces. The brand's "black", softer than true black.
- **Pearl Canvas** (pearl-canvas): the page background site-wide, the hero's mobile copy area, and the text on filled graphite buttons.
- **Gallery White** (gallery-white): the Collection only. The four packshots are retouched onto pure white so the bottles stand on it like a gallery wall, after the La Prairie reference.

### Secondary
- **Caviar Greige** (caviar-greige): the caviar section, sampled from the tinted caviar macro so the photograph and the facts below it are one surface.
- **Ritual Linen** (ritual-linen) / **Ritual Stone** (ritual-stone) / **Ritual Taupe** (ritual-taupe): The Ritual's editorial board only: the text panel, the step tiles, and the italic "Ritual" in the title.
- **Platinum** (platinum) / **Silver Mist** (silver-mist): labels, hover and focus on the dark footer.

### Neutral
- **Pearl Band** (pearl-band): image placeholders while photographs load.
- **Body Graphite** (body-graphite) / **Reading Graphite** (reading-graphite): body and lede copy on light surfaces (10:1 and above).
- **Muted Graphite** (muted-graphite): tertiary notes and captions (6:1 on pearl; the floor for small text).
- **Charcoal** (charcoal): text on light photographic backdrops.
- **Porcelain** (porcelain): type set on darker photographs (the clinical portrait's shade, the Matterhorn), and the footer's headings.
- **Footer Blue-Black** (footer-blue-black): the footer, a deliberate blue-black rather than neutral.
- **On-dark Text** (on-dark-text) / **On-dark Muted** (on-dark-muted): footer copy.

### Tertiary
- **Swiss Red** (swiss-red): the cross in the logo only.

**The Sampled Warmth Rule.** No hue is chosen for the UI. A section may take a warm tone only when it is sampled from (or retouched into) its own photograph, so the image and the surface meet without a seam.

**The Punctuation Rule.** Dark surfaces appear only in the footer and behind photography. A text-only section is never dark.

**The One Red Rule.** Swiss red belongs to the logo's cross. It never colours a button, link, badge or price.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Cormorant Garamond (with Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Inter (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Arial)

**Character:** A fine, high-contrast serif carries the brand's voice in light weights. A calm, neutral sans carries everything she needs to read or tap. The serif never sets body copy, and the sans never sets a headline.

### Hierarchy
On the homepage every shared size is one CSS token on `body.cos-h26` (`--t-heading`, `--t-title`, `--t-numeral`, `--t-figure`, `--t-body`, `--t-detail`, `--t-small`, plus `--s-heading` for the gap under a heading; end of home-2026.css). The older `--h26-type-*` names alias them, so a role always renders at one size per breakpoint.
- **Display** (300, 2.5rem phones rising to 3.75rem at 1440px, 1.06): the hero line only.
- **Display feature** (400, 3.5rem phones / 4.5rem tablet / up to 6rem desktop, 0.98): The Ritual's "The / *Ritual*" board title, the one other display moment.
- **Heading** (300, sentence case, 2.125rem phones / 2.5rem tablet / 3rem desktop, 1.12, -0.01em): every section heading: The Collection, Caviar in every formula, Clinical evidence, and the Swiss heritage line "*Fewer things, made well.*" (set in italic). A 20px gap below (24px desktop).
- **Figure** (300, 3rem phones / 3.5rem desktop, 1, lining + tabular): clinical results.
- **Numeral** (400, 2rem phones / 2.5rem from tablet, lining): the ritual step numbers 1–4.
- **Title** (400, 1.25rem phones / 1.375rem from tablet): product names, ritual verbs, and the species name *Acipenser baerii* (italic).
- **Body** (300 for editorial ledes, 400 for instructions and study details, 1rem, 1.5–1.75): all reading copy. Measure 40–65ch. On darker photographs, one weight step up.
- **Detail** (400, 0.9375rem): size · price, shipping and returns, buttons.
- **Small** (400, 0.875rem): tertiary notes only (the portrait credit).
- **Label** (500, 0.75rem, 0.18–0.24em tracking, uppercase): section labels, kickers, tags, footer column heads.
- **Action** (500, 0.9375rem, 0.01em, sentence case): buttons and text links, with a trailing arrow.

**The Short Caps Rule.** Spaced caps are only for labels of four words or fewer. Anything longer is sentence case.

**The Lining Numeral Rule.** Any numeral set in Cormorant (figures, step numbers, "24h") uses lining numerals; old-style figures read as "o1".

**The Reading Floor Rule.** Nothing she has to read is under 16px. Purchase details and actions sit at 15px; only labels and tertiary notes go smaller.

## Layout

The homepage is a single column of full-width sections, each with one focus: hero → Collection → Caviar → Clinical evidence → Swiss heritage → The Ritual → footer.
- **Container:** 1240–1360px, with a 22px phone gutter, 40px from tablet and about 6vw on desktop.
- **Breakpoints:** 700px (tablet), 900px (story copy beside imagery), 1100px (desktop hero art direction; clinical leader lines), 1280px (caviar copy moves onto its photograph).
- **Section rhythm:** about 72px vertical padding on phones and 96–112px on desktop. Full-bleed photographic sections carry no padding of their own.
- **Compositions:**
  - Full-bleed photograph with type on its calm side: hero, caviar, clinical evidence, Swiss heritage. Below the breakpoint the copy moves above or below the photograph, or the plate grows with the copy, so text never spills off the image.
  - A centred column over a 4-up grid (2×2 on phones): the Collection.
  - An editorial board on a 6px gutter grid: The Ritual (text panel, portrait, four step tiles, offer band).
- **Anchors:** in-page anchors carry `scroll-margin-top` to clear the 80px fixed header.

**The First Screen Rule.** On a 390×844 iPhone, the hero headline and its one action sit in the first screen, with the product photograph beginning below them.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by design. There are no box-shadows on UI. Depth comes from photography (pearl silk, chrome, backlit caviar, cloud), from tonal shifts between pearl, white and the photo-matched warm tones, and from hairline rules at 10–20% graphite. Where a photograph would otherwise show its edge against the page, it is feathered with a CSS mask (the Collection packshots) or its backdrop is extended in the image itself (the hero silk, the clinical plate).

**The Flat Surface Rule.** If something needs separating, use space first, a hairline second, and a tone change third. Never a shadow.

**The No Frame Rule.** A photograph never sits in a visible box on the page: match its backdrop, extend it, or feather it.

## Shapes

Square. Buttons, inputs, images and sections all have 0 radius. The only exceptions are the 2px radius on the "Self-reported" tag, and circles used as meaning: the clinical marker dots and the numbered markers on phones. Rules are 1px hairlines, often short: a 56px rule under section headings, rules either side of The Ritual's offer, and the leader lines that join a clinical figure to the skin it describes.

## Components

### Buttons
Square and understated; one action per view.
- **Shape:** square corners (0), 54px tall, 30px horizontal padding; full width in the phone hero.
- **Primary (filled):** graphite ink with pearl text. The Ritual's "Shop the Complete Ritual" and the phone hero.
- **Outline:** graphite text and a 1px graphite border on the desktop hero's light photograph; it fills graphite on hover.
- **Shop now:** the Collection's per-product action, a 44px outlined button at the detail size; it fills graphite when the card is hovered.
- **Hover / Focus:** a one-step fill change and a 5px arrow slide over 0.35s; focus is a 2px outline at 4px offset, graphite on light and porcelain on photographs.

### Text links
- **Style:** Inter 500 with a trailing arrow and a 44px tap height; graphite on light, porcelain on photographs. The underline draws in on hover.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** square, 44px high, a hairline border on a dark raised fill (footer newsletter). Text stays 16px so iOS never zooms.
- **Feedback:** a persistent status line announces pending, success and error; on error the email stays, focus returns, and a contact route is offered.

### Collection card
- **Structure:** the packshot on white (4:5, feathered), the product name in serif over two lines, one benefit line, size · price, and a Shop now button. All four buttons align across the row.

### Clinical callout (signature component)
- **Desktop:** a marker dot on the skin, a hairline leader to the figure, then the product, figure, finding, "● Instrument-measured", panel and method.
- **Phones:** numbered circles on the face, keyed to a list beneath.
- **Self-reported results** are never pinned to the face: a separate caption with a "Self-reported" tag, plus "Illustrative portrait; not a study participant."

### Ritual step tile (signature component)
- **Structure:** a serif numeral and verb (1 Cleanse, 2 Treat, 3 Revive, 4 Restore), a photograph of a model using that formula, and one or two sentences on how to use it. On small phones each tile becomes a row, photo beside text.

### Navigation
- **Desktop header:** a fixed 80px bar: tracked-caps links left, the centred logo, help, account and bag right. Over the light hero it stays transparent with graphite links; on scroll it becomes a 94% pearl bar with a hairline.
- **Mobile:** a menu button, the logo and the bag, on pearl.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every section light; set type on the calm side of a photograph rather than in a separate band.
- **Do** sample any warm surface tone from its own photograph, and keep the Collection on white.
- **Do** name every clinical figure with its product, method and panel, and keep self-reported results visibly secondary and unpinned.
- **Do** keep tap targets at 44px or more and reading copy at 16px or more.
- **Do** use lining numerals for any Cormorant numeral.
- **Do** photograph the real packaging with accurate labels; product imagery is the ornament.
- **Do** lead with feeling on the homepage; certificates, methods and standards live on the science pages.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add shadows, rounded corners or gradient fills to UI elements.
- **Don't** let a photograph show a frame or seam against the page.
- **Don't** use Swiss red anywhere except the logo.
- **Don't** set long phrases in spaced caps.
- **Don't** add star ratings, testimonials or press logos.
- **Don't** echo cobalt blue with caviar (another house's signature).
