## Direction

The product must feel photographed with the care of a jewellery campaign: an accurate white 24h jar, cool chrome, precise glass edges, luminous ivory, pale champagne reflected light, and large clean negative space. Avoid making every section beige. Ivory campaign / gallery white collection / near-black ingredient interlude / white evidence produces a deliberate photographic rhythm. Keep the red Swiss cross as the only saturated identity detail. Do not add physical diamonds or jewellery simply to signify expense.

## Missing imagery and proposed exact generation/edit prompt

Priority: new hero landscape and independently composed portrait hero. Second priority: lake/stone/architecture provenance editorial with verifiable geographic origin, or use the authentic existing production photograph. Never generate a fictitious brand facility or documentary evidence.

Proposed image edit, using `cream.jpg` as the packaging reference (not generated hxh labels):

> Create a photoreal luxury skincare campaign photograph using the supplied Caviar of Switzerland 24h Regeneration Cream product photograph as the exact packaging authority. Preserve the actual jar silhouette, white inner container, transparent glass outer wall and base thickness, polished silver chrome closed lid, all original silver label typography, original red Swiss cross, product name, and printed 50 ml volume. Do not redesign, relabel, embellish or invent any packaging detail. Single closed jar in the right-hand 45% of a very wide horizontal frame, jar approximately 35% of total frame width, viewed straight on at a subtle elevated angle with full jar and cap visible. It stands on a polished warm ivory stone surface with a physically believable soft reflection; the reflection fades naturally. Background is a seamless luminous ivory-to-pale-champagne architectural light field with one broad soft sunlit diagonal and subtle liquid-like reflected light, substantial clean quiet negative space across the left 45% reserved for real website text. Studio large-format product photography, exact glass refraction, carefully controlled black-and-white chrome reflections, crisp brand label, tactile expensive materials, rich highlight rolloff, natural soft shadow, no hard white cutout halo. Minimal composition with the product as a precious object. No jewellery, no diamonds, no pearls, no caviar props, no flowers, no fabric, no pedestal steps, no mountain landscape, no extra product, no text outside the product, no interface or borders. Output landscape at least 2400 pixels wide. The supplied product must remain immediately recognisable and commercially accurate.

Portrait followup: same photographic world, product centered in lower-middle at 65% frame width, complete jar visible, upper 35% quiet ivory space for HTML copy. Reference exact generated landscape + original packshot so packaging does not drift. Inspect labels against original before shipping. If accurate generation cannot preserve the brand, retain original photograph and use a generated environment only, with a truthful product composite and recorded provenance.
